The Founder's Story

From Curious Child to India's Favourite Magician

✦

It all started with a simple card trick Parth saw at age 8 that left him completely speechless. That sense of wonder never left him. By 14, he was performing at school functions; by 18, at weddings and corporate dinners across Mumbai.

After studying performing arts and apprenticing under legendary illusionist Mahendra Trivedi, Parth launched Magician Parth in 2009 — with a single mission: to give every audience member that same feeling of pure astonishment he felt as a child.

Today, Parth is one of India's most sought-after event entertainers, blending classic sleight of hand with cutting-edge mentalism and grand stage illusions. Every performance is a unique story — crafted just for your audience.
